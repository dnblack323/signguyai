## Post-Installation Care

### First 48 Hours - CRITICAL PERIOD

⚠️ **DO NOT WASH OR WET** the application area for at least 48 hours after installation.

- Allow adhesive to fully bond
- Avoid touching or pressing on decal
- Keep out of rain if possible
- Do not apply any cleaning products

---

## Cleaning Your Decals

### Hand Washing (Recommended)
1. **Wait 48 hours** after installation
2. Use mild soap and warm water
3. Soft cloth or sponge only
4. Gently pat or wipe - do not scrub
5. Rinse with clean water
6. Pat dry with soft towel

### Pressure Washing
- ✅ OK after 48 hours if used carefully
- Keep nozzle at least **12 inches away**
- Use **low pressure** (under 1200 PSI)
- Spray at **90-degree angle** (perpendicular to surface)
- ❌ Never spray directly at decal edges

---

## What to AVOID

❌ **Abrasive materials:** Steel wool, scratch pads, rough brushes  
❌ **Harsh chemicals:** Bleach, acetone, strong solvents  
❌ **Automatic car wash brushes:** Can lift edges  
❌ **Waxing over decals:** May cause discoloration  
❌ **Scraping or picking:** Even if edge lifts slightly  

---

## Spot Cleaning

For dirt, bugs, or sticky residue:

1. Soak area with warm soapy water
2. Let sit for 2-3 minutes
3. Gently wipe with soft cloth
4. **Stubborn spots:** Use isopropyl alcohol (70%) on cloth
5. Rinse and dry

---

## Lifespan & Durability

**Indoor decals:** 5-7 years  
**Outdoor decals (with UV protection):** 3-5 years  
**Factors:** Sun exposure, weather, surface type, care

---

## Signs of Wear

Normal aging includes:
- Slight fading after years of sun exposure
- Minor edge lifting after several years

**Contact {{company_name}} if you notice:**
- Excessive bubbling
- Premature peeling or lifting
- Color fading within first year
- Adhesive failure
