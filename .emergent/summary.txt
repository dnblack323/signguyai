<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
The user wants to build the MVP of a web-based sign-shop operating system called Sign Guy AI (originally Signtists Lab AI).

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**
The application should be a single platform for sign shops to manage Customers, Jobs, Invoices, Productivity, Financials, and Employee Time/Payroll. It must replace disconnected systems like spreadsheets and emails.

**Core Modules (MVP):**
1.  **Customer Management:** CRUD for customers (name, company, contact, status, notes).
2.  **Quotes/Estimates:** Create quotes tied to a customer, with line items, status, and one-click conversion to a Job.
3.  **Job/Order Tickets:** Create jobs tied to customers, with a name, description, status, and due date.
4.  **Invoicing (MVP):** Create invoices from jobs, track status, and allow manual payment marking.
5.  **Productivity & Scheduling:** Task list, calendar view, and Kanban board for jobs.
6.  **Financial Tracking (Basic):** Simple entry for daily sales and expenses.
7.  **Time Clock & Payroll (MVP):** A critical module for employees to clock in/out.
8.  **AI Tools Suite (MVP):** A suite of AI tools for design, branding, and business tasks.
9.  **Webstores (Fundraiser & B2B):** A comprehensive module for creating B2B, Fundraiser, and Creator webstores with a master product catalog, public-facing storefronts, and order management integrated into the main job system.
