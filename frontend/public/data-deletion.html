Privacy & Data Rights

Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: April 25, 2026

SignGuy AI OS provides users and business tenants with a way to request deletion of account data and third-party integration data, including Facebook/Meta integration data.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion, email:

support@signguy-ai.com

Please include:

  • Your name
  • Business name
  • Login email used for SignGuy AI OS
  • Connected Facebook Page name, if applicable
  • The type of data you want deleted
  • Any additional details needed to identify your account

What Happens After Your Request

After receiving a valid deletion request, we will verify account ownership when needed, disconnect related third-party integrations, delete stored integration tokens, and delete or anonymize stored integration data where legally and technically permitted.

Self-Service Disconnect

Users may also disconnect integrations from: Settings > Integrations

Data That May Be Retained

Some records may be retained when required for billing, security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, completed transactions, or audit logs.

Contact

For questions about this policy or to submit a data request, email support@signguy-ai.com.
