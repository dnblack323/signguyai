## First 7 Days After Installation - CRITICAL PERIOD

⚠️ **DO NOT WASH your vehicle for at least 7 days** after installation to allow the adhesive to cure properly.

- Avoid automatic car washes
- Avoid pressure washers
- Avoid extreme temperatures when possible
- Park in shade or garage if available
- Do not apply wax or protectants

---

## Washing Your Wrapped Vehicle

### Recommended Method: Hand Washing
1. Use clean water and mild automotive soap (pH neutral, no wax)
2. Use soft sponge or microfiber cloth
3. Rinse thoroughly with clean water
4. Dry with clean microfiber towel or chamois
5. **Water temperature:** Cool to lukewarm only (never hot)

### Touchless/Brushless Car Washes
- ✅ Acceptable if water pressure is below 2000 PSI
- ✅ Keep nozzle at least 12 inches away from wrap
- ✅ Angle spray at 90 degrees to surface (not at seams/edges)
- ❌ Avoid high-pressure washes on edges and seams

### Automatic Car Washes with Brushes
- ❌ **NOT RECOMMENDED** - brushes can lift edges and scratch wrap
- If absolutely necessary, use only soft-cloth systems

---

## Spot Cleaning

For bugs, bird droppings, tree sap, or tar:

1. Soak area with warm soapy water for several minutes
2. Use soft cloth to gently wipe away
3. **For stubborn spots:** Use denatured alcohol or 2-propanol (isopropyl) with soft cloth
4. Rinse immediately with clean water
5. **Never scrape or use abrasive tools**

---

## What to AVOID

❌ **Abrasive products:** Scratch pads, brushes, harsh chemicals  
❌ **Waxes & polishes:** Can damage wrap finish  
❌ **Petroleum-based cleaners:** Will harm vinyl  
❌ **Engine degreasers:** Too harsh for wrap material  
❌ **High pressure at seams:** Can lift wrap edges  
❌ **Parking under trees:** Sap and droppings are corrosive  
❌ **Extended sun exposure:** Park in shade when possible  

---

## Fuel Spills

If fuel spills on wrap:
1. Wipe off **immediately** with clean cloth
2. Rinse area with water quickly
3. Do not let fuel sit on wrap

---
