# Sign Guy AI - Complete Feature List & Roadmap v2

## 🚀 CURRENT FEATURES (MVP - Available Now)

### 📊 Dashboard
- Real-time business stats at a glance
- Today's revenue, pending jobs, overdue invoices
- Recent activity feed with quick navigation
- Quick action buttons for common tasks

### 👥 Customer Management
- Full customer database (add, edit, delete)
- Contact information storage (phone, email, address)
- Customer status tracking (active, inactive, lead)
- Search and filter customers
- Customer notes and history
- View all related quotes, jobs, and invoices per customer

### 📝 Quotes & Estimates
- Create detailed quotes with line items
- Automatic total calculations
- Quote status tracking (draft, sent, approved, rejected)
- **One-click convert quote to job**
- Quote detail popup for quick viewing
- Duplicate quotes for similar projects

### 🔧 Job/Order Management
- Create jobs from quotes or scratch
- **Multiple line items per job** with:
  - Item types (banner, yard sign, vehicle wrap, decals, etc.)
  - Quantity and unit pricing
  - Per-item status tracking (pending, in production, done)
  - Item notes
- Job status workflow (pending → in progress → completed)
- Due date tracking
- Add notes to jobs
- **Schedule jobs directly to calendar**
- Kanban board view for visual job management

### 💰 Invoicing
- Create invoices from completed jobs
- Line items automatically pulled from job
- Invoice status tracking (draft, sent, paid, overdue)
- **Mark invoices as paid** with payment date
- Invoice preview modal
- Invoice history per customer

### ⏰ Time Clock & Attendance
- Employee clock in/out
- Break tracking (start break, end break)
- **Sequence validation** (can't end shift before starting)
- Daily shift summaries
- Hours worked calculations
- Real-time clock status display

### 💵 Payroll Management
- Track employee earnings
- Record advances/loans
- Process payroll payments
- **Running balance calculation** (Earnings - Advances - Payments)
- Payroll reports by employee
- Transaction history

### 📈 Financial Tracking
- **Daily sales entry** with payment method (Cash, Credit, Check)
- Expense tracking with 15+ categories
- Tax collected tracking
- Financial summaries

### ✅ Productivity & Scheduling
- Task management (create, edit, complete tasks)
- **Calendar view** for scheduling
- **Kanban board** for job workflow
- Task priorities and due dates
- Link tasks to specific jobs

### 🤖 AI Tools Suite (15 Tools)
- Design Tools: Photo Enhancer, Image Vectorizer, Font Identifier, AI Sign Designer, AI Banner Designer, Mockup Creator
- Branding Tools: Logo Creator, Branding Kit Generator
- Business Tools: Business Copywriter, Document Composer, Pricing Intelligence Assistant
- Marketing Tools: Social Media Job Post Creator, Social Media Pack Generator, Content Calendar Creator, Campaign Builder

### 🏪 Webstores (Fundraiser & B2B)
- Three store types: Business (B2B), Fundraiser, Creator
- Master Product Catalog with variants (size, color)
- Custom branding per store (logo, colors)
- Public shareable storefronts
- Shopping cart and checkout
- Order management and payout tracking
