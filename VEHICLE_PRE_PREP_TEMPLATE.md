## Pre-Wrap Cleaning Protocol

### Step 1: Exterior Wash
- Use automotive soap (pH neutral)
- Hand wash entire vehicle
- Rinse thoroughly
- Dry completely

### Step 2: Surface Prep
- Wipe down with isopropyl alcohol (70% or higher)
- Pay special attention to edges, seams, and recessed areas
- Remove all wax, polish, or sealant residue
- Clay bar if needed for contaminants

### Step 3: Final Inspection
- Surface should be completely dry
- No dust, debris, or contaminants
- Paint surface smooth to touch
- Ready for vinyl application
